How 1on1 works

From finding an expert to joining your session.

1 · Find your expert

Browse the catalog by category, rating, and price. Each host lists their session types — with durations and prices — and their real weekly availability, so what you see is bookable.

2 · Pick a slot

Choose a session type and a time from the host's calendar. Times are shown in the host's timezone and double-booked slots are rejected server-side, so a confirmed slot is truly yours.

3 · Check out

Pricing is computed server-side from the session's listed price: a platform fee is added and any promotion is applied before payment. The total you approve is the total you pay.

4 · Pay securely

Card payments run through Stripe's PaymentSheet — we never see or store your card details. Your booking is confirmed the moment your payment is captured, and both you and the host can see it.

5 · Join the session

A private video room is created for your booking and opens with the session. Join with one tap from the session card in the app. Afterward, leave a review to help others pick great hosts.

Cancellations

Cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before the start time. Inside 24 hours the booking is non-refundable unless your host agrees otherwise. If a host doesn't attend a paid session, contact support@1on1.app within 48 hours for a full refund or rebooking. Details in the Terms of Service.
