Support

Answers to the questions we hear most.

Accounts

How do I create an account? Tap "Continue to sign in" in the app, then "New here? Sign up". You'll confirm your email and accept the Terms and Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account? In-app: Profile → Account deletion. Deleting removes personal identifiers as described in the Privacy Policy; booking and payment records are retained where law requires. You can also request deletion via support@1on1.app.

Bookings

Can I reschedule? Cancel the booking (free up to 24 hours before) and rebook the time that works. Your host can also coordinate changes over in-app messages.

The host didn't show up. Contact support@1on1.app within 48 hours for a full refund or rebooking.

Payments & refunds

When am I charged? At checkout, when you confirm the booking. The total — session price, platform fee, minus any promotion — is shown before you pay.

How do refunds work? Cancellations more than 24 hours before the start time are refunded in full to your original payment method. Details in the Terms.

Video sessions

When does my room open? Your private video room opens with the session from the session card in the app — no downloads, it runs in your browser.

Are sessions recorded? Not by default, and never without the consent of both participants.
