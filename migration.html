The migration playbook

TIBCO to Spring Boot, without stopping the business

We never propose a "big-bang" rewrite — the riskiest thing you can do to a production integration estate. Instead we use the strangler-fig approach: the new platform grows around the old one, flow by flow, until the old one naturally disappears.

Phase 1Discover

We inventory every integration in your TIBCO estate: what each one does, which systems it touches, how business-critical it is, and what it costs to run.

In plain English: before we move anything, we draw the complete map of how your systems talk to each other — so nothing gets forgotten or broken.

Phase 2Design the target

We design the Spring Boot target state: service boundaries, REST APIs, event streams, and the delivery pipeline. Every decision is written down and reviewed with your architects.

In plain English: we draw what the modern replacement looks like — and your team approves every page before any code is written.

Phase 3Pilot

We migrate one carefully chosen flow end-to-end: TIBCO process → Spring Boot service, with automated regression tests proving identical behavior on real production payloads.

In plain English: we move one small, safe piece first and prove the whole approach works before touching anything important.

Phase 4Strangle & cut over

Flows move one at a time, old and new running side by side. Traffic switches gradually, with automated comparison and an instant rollback path for every step.

In plain English: the new platform takes over piece by piece. If anything misbehaves, we flip back to the old path in seconds — not days.

Phase 5Enable & hand over

Your engineers have been pairing since week one. We finish with documentation, runbooks, training, and a support window — then get out of the way. No permanent retainer.

In plain English: we make sure your own people can run everything confidently, and the license meter finally stops running.

Why Spring Boot?

  • No license meter. Spring Boot is open source — no per-core, per-flow, or per-message fees that grow every year.
  • The talent pool is enormous. Java and Spring are among the most widely known skills in enterprise software; hiring is easier and cheaper than niche middleware expertise.
  • The ecosystem is battle-tested. Spring Boot backs some of the world's largest production estates, with security patches and releases you can plan around.
  • You own it. The code, the pipelines, the platform — all in your repositories, under your control.

What we won't do

✅ What you get from us

  • Incremental, provable migration steps
  • Automated tests as the safety net
  • Open documents your team can read
  • Your engineers involved from day one
  • A clean end: you own the platform

🚫 What we refuse to do

  • Big-bang rewrites with a fixed cutover weekend
  • Black-box consulting nobody can later maintain
  • Replacing TIBCO with a different lock-in
  • Endless retainers with no handover
  • Moving flows without regression evidence
