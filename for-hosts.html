Host on 1on1

Turn your expertise into a schedule — we run the infrastructure.

What you get

  • Your own catalog — define session types with durations and prices; clients book exactly what you offer.
  • Availability that respects your calendar — weekly rules in your timezone; the platform prevents double-booking automatically.
  • Payments handled — clients pay up front through Stripe; cancellations and refunds follow the published policy.
  • Video rooms included — a private room is created for every confirmed booking and opens with the session.
  • Client messaging — coordinate agendas and prep notes inside the app.
  • Reviews that compound — completed sessions collect ratings that make your listing stand out.

What we expect

  • Show up on time, every time — your availability is a commitment.
  • Provide the sessions you list at the prices you list.
  • Keep professional conduct; the Terms apply to everyone.
  • You're an independent provider: you're responsible for your own taxes and compliance.

Getting started

Hosting onboards by invitation while the platform is in soft launch. Email support@1on1.app with a short bio, the services you'd offer, and your typical hourly rate, and we'll set up your host profile.

Payouts: during soft launch, host payouts are settled off-platform after each completed session. In-app payouts via Stripe Connect are on the roadmap.
