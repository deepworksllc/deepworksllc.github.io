About

DEEP Works

Enterprise integration specialists. We make open source the safe choice for organizations that can't afford downtime.

DEEP Works was founded on a simple observation: enterprise integration platforms are the hardest software to replace and the easiest to overpay for. Proprietary middleware like TIBCO sits at the center of a company's operations — and over the years, the license invoices grow while the original architects move on.

We exist to change that. Our practice focuses on one thing done well: helping organizations modernize from proprietary integration stacks to open-source Spring Boot — with methods that let the business keep running every single day of the transition.

How we work

🔍 Evidence over promises

Every migrated flow is proven by automated regression tests against real production payloads — before it takes traffic.

📈 Small steps, always reversible

No big-bang cutovers. The strangler-fig approach means every step has a rollback path measured in seconds.

📖 Nothing is a black box

Architecture decisions, migration plans, and runbooks live in your repositories, readable by your whole team.

🤝 An end, by design

Your engineers pair with ours from week one. The engagement ends with your team owning the platform — not with a bigger retainer.
